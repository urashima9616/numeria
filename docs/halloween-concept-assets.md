# Halloween concept assets

Generated 2026-10-15 with the built-in ImageGen tool, one call per concept. No API/CLI fallback. These four images are **design references only**, outside Unity Resources. They do not add playable worlds or production creature sprites. Originals are retained at their generated paths.

The two evolution boards use `unity/Assets/Resources/generated/mushmellow_large_icon.png` as a style reference only. The environment concepts were generated from text, without an input image. Original game art was inspected before writing the prompts.

Visual review: the environments establish distinct palettes and landmarks, but their painted stairs/doors do not prove collision connectivity. The mansion contains friendly ghost decorations despite the no-monsters prompt; they are scenery concepts, not roster entries. The family boards show distinct silhouettes and retained faces, but have broad glow and tight edge margins. Before production, generate individual portraits with clean isolated alpha and padding, test at actual UI sizes, and do not use a three-character board as one battle sprite. The pumpkin family adopted a pumpkin head plus pumpkin torso rather than a single torso-face; use the approved concept consistently if proceeding.
