## Runtime assembly

`WorldScene` supplies per-biome ground, river/chasm/lava, cliffs, trees/pillars, encounter vegetation, landmarks, bridges, restoration marks and atmospheric drift. The existing semantic gameplay grid and chest/discovery identities remain unchanged. Forest keeps its previous authored events and resources.

`SpellChoreography` slices the three VFX material sheets into four regions each and animates them with separate motion grammars. Runtime numbers and shapes come from puzzle data; generated art never contains the answer. These sheets are materials, not pre-rendered animation sheets.

Row1 left mossy timber vine bridge; row1 right snow-dusted timber bridge. Row2 left ivory marble bridge with slender golden railings; row2 right ochre sandstone bridge with blue inlay. The bridge props in the original biome atlases are retained as source art but are not used for gameplay crossings. Visual QA showed their isometric perspective did not align with cardinal movement, so `WorldScene.Bridges` uses the six top-down bridge cuts and places one deck per connected bridge component. Forest retains its previously accepted vine bridge. Count Crunch uses the standalone mandible image rather than paw art.
