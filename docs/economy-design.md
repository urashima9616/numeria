# Numeria Exploration Economy

## Goals

- Math is part of exploration: every map has four visible number runes, and every rune asks a themed puzzle
  before it becomes a one-time coin pickup.
- Coins reward both curiosity and battles. Wrong puzzle answers never consume the rune or remove coins.
- Shops are optional power and convenience, not a mandatory progression toll.
- Merchant stock is permanently limited per save so a child cannot accidentally create an infinite grind loop.

## Income

| Source | Mystic Forest | Silent Peaks | Azure Sky City |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Four discovery runes | 20 total | 34 total | 47 total |
| Ordinary battle | 3–6 | 4–8 | 5–10 |
| Merchant challenge | 16 | 22 | 28 |
| Map boss | 30 | 40 | 50 |

Enemy coins are intentionally small and variable. Discovery and guardian rewards are fixed so important
milestones remain predictable for a five-year-old.

## Limited shops

Each map's merchant must first be defeated in a trainer battle. Their partner is stronger and tougher than a
same-level wild Mathmon, cannot be caught, has no boss shield, and gives no random item drop. After victory the
merchant remains on the map and opens the shop on contact.

| Map | Consumables | Accessory | Evolution Stone |
|---|---|---|---:|
| Mystic Forest | Potion 6 ×3; snack 8 ×2 | ATK +1, 16 ×1 | 32 ×1 |
| Silent Peaks | Potion 9 ×4; snack 11 ×3 | DEF +1, 22 ×1 | 45 ×1 |
| Azure Sky City | Potion 12 ×4; snack 15 ×3 | ATK +1 / DEF +1, 30 ×1 | 65 ×1 |

The first map's four discoveries plus merchant victory provide 36 coins, enough for its evolution stone or a
useful mix of consumables without requiring repeated battles. Later stones ask the player to combine exploration,
the merchant challenge and some normal play. Accessories cap at +2 combined stats and one copy per shop; this
keeps the existing ATK/DEF damage curve meaningful.
